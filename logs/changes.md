## 2026-10-25: Phase 3 research and verification built and tested

**Built (`src/news/`):**
- `feeds.py`: reads the 19 feeds in `config/sources.json` and keeps only items inside the Boston news day, never after "now". Raw text goes to the git-ignored `cache/`.
- `cluster.py`: groups duplicate headlines and ranks events by independent outlets. Writes `logs/research/<date>/candidates.json` (titles, links and times only).
- `brief.py`: blocks an entry on:
  - a future leak
  - fewer than 2 independent outlets for the focal story (each wire service counts once; AP and AFP count separately)
  - non-https links or missing timestamps
  - public figures without a verified action
  - a story whose sources are all from another Boston day
- `article_meta.py`: confirms publication time from the article page. Respects robots.txt; at most 30 fetches per run.
- `wiki_events.py`: for past dates, reads Wikipedia's Current Events page as it existed at the date's cutoff.

**Feeds:**
- 18 of the 19 listed feeds work. France 24 currently serves a bot-check page instead of its feed; it is reported, not bypassed.
- Not listed: WaPo (502 error), and CNN and WSJ (stale feeds).

**Tests:** 34 checks pass. They include:
- no future leak
- daylight-saving day = 25 hours
- wire-copy counting
- wrong-day detection

**Live dry run, 2026-10-25** (`logs/research/2026-10-25/`): 258 items → 25 candidates.
- Chosen: focal = Serbia's Vučić conceding to the student-led movement (AP + AFP verified; BBC and NYT confirmed via feed headlines); supporting = Machado's return to Venezuela.
- Left out: Brazil's runoff (still counting); a single-source tent fire (not drawn).

**Historical tests:**
- 2026-10-12: Nobel economics prize as focal, with 3 independent sources timestamped via page metadata; hurricanes as single-source supporting. The brief passes.
- 2026-10-20: discovery worked. Found that JAXA's MMX launch is filed under Oct 20 on Wikipedia (Japan date) but happened at 3:41 p.m. Boston time on Oct 19. The checker now blocks such wrong-day stories.

**Limits found:**
- BBC, Guardian, DW and CNN article pages can't be fetched from here, and NYT is paywalled. Those were confirmed via feed headlines and summaries only.
- Reuters, France 24, nobelprize.org, SCMP and geo.tv forbid automated reading in robots.txt; those pages were skipped.
- Historical verification fetches pages from many sites. The cloud workspace's network setting will need a wider allowlist or full access; this is a decision for Phase 5.

**Usage (approx.):**
- Live run: 19 feed requests, 6 article reads (4 more were blocked or failed).
- Historical runs: 2 Wikipedia requests, ~10 timestamp checks, 2 searches, 3 article reads.
